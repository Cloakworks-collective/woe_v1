## Designed (spec/) — unchanged summary

- **Buildings** — 13 levelled civilian + counted housing/barracks; pop growth 1→100/day; cost ratio bands; storage = 20k × level protected; instant builds
- **Research** — 10 fields × 5 levels, exponential costs (×5/level), specialization forced
- **Market** — anonymous Grand Bazaar, player-set asks, cheapest-first fills, caravans 1k × Market Square level
- **Combat** — raid/siege/revenge/bombard (10 action turns, instantaneous); 4-phase battles; wall integrity; scattering (<30% troops, exempt <500 pop); XP bands (refusal ≥75% up, −5 below 50%); ±10% luck
- **Siege** — War Foundry 1–10, five offense/counter pairs, counters −75%
- **Espionage** — 5 Tradecraft ops, lodge-level catch gating, caught spies executed, ±20% luck
- **Clans** — 5 roles; storage (3× cap) → hall (tax shelter ≤50%) → wonder (−10/20/30%); wars (+100% dmg, +200 net kills → XP transfer + tribute, 48h truce); churn (forfeit, 48h, 2/era)
- **Victory** — eras named after winners; Overlord (72h cum + 12h straight, 10k floor) / Clan (150k floor); visible-empire ranking
- **Races** — sum-zero table, Humans baseline, Gnolls spymasters
